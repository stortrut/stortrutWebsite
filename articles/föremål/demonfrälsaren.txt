[h1]
Demonfrälsaren

Ett långsvärd (longsword) som används av Hektor Kostos. 
 
The wide blade of this dwarvish sword has a blue-orange shine to it. Its hilt and cross-guard is made out of an heavy deep blue jade.
 
[h2]
Funktion
This sword deals an additional 1d6 radiant damage the first time you hit a demon, undead, or fiend in a combat. This creature is now marked until you take a long rest.
 
You can as an action, once a day, point this sword at a marked creature within 60 feet and say “I give you mercy”/“Jag skonar dig” to deal 3d10 radiant damage.
 
If a cleric of @Karoon is attuned and wielding this weapon, attack rolls of 19 are also considered Critical Hits.
