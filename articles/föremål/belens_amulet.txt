[h2]
Amulet of Belen

[quote]
Wondrous item, requires attunement

This old polished bronze amulet has 3 softly smiling skulls etched into the metal. Each skull can hold a spell.

As an action you can cast a spell, with the casting time of one action, into one of the skulls. The skull retains the spell for 6 hour.
After you go down to 0 hit points, the most recent spell that was cast into a skull, is cast on the wearer.
[end_quote]
